# Simply Mirrors 0.1.3

## Complementary motion blur and temporal stability

- Reconstruct the actual previous reflected camera view, projection and eye for each mirror plane. Preserve Iris/Oculus origin-relative previous-camera uniforms instead of forcing zero camera velocity.
- Keep the real main-camera shader, framebuffer, depth/stencil, and temporal state isolated from reflected rendering; reset reflected camera history when switching worlds.
- Add a real Minecraft Complementary Reimagined r5.9.3 camera-pan/strafe acceptance test with motion blur and TAA explicitly enabled, including reflected displacement and ghosting checks.

## Large mirror scenes

- Batch coplanar mirror faces and greedily merge adjacent visible blocks into exact rectangles, retaining full-resolution reflection sampling and respecting holes and occluders.
- Apply the 16,384 visible-face budget after screen-facing and exposure checks on all supported versions, rather than truncating raw nearby candidates.
- Reuse native vertex staging storage, reduce transient projection allocations and cache merged geometry across camera movement, distance re-sorting and periodic world rescans.
- Invalidate cached meshes for actual geometry changes, including same-count edits, duplicate faces and changed normals.

## Verification and compatibility

- Retain all eight Fabric/Forge/NeoForge targets: Minecraft 1.16.5, 1.20.1, 1.21.1 and 26.1.2.
- Extend Java and native OpenGL coverage for previous-camera history, cross-world resets, 22,000-candidate face selection, exact RGBA parity, depth/stencil restoration, sparse and multi-plane geometry, and moving-camera mesh-cache rebuild counts.
- Preserve Complementary and Euphoria shader compatibility acceptance and all six mirror orientations.
- Carry forward the unreleased 0.1.2 shader framebuffer/depth-stencil compatibility fixes; see [0.1.2 notes](0.1.2.md) for details.

**Performance note:** Isolated Mesa software-OpenGL compositor benchmarks show reduced frame times and steady-state native vertex allocations. These are not end-to-end Minecraft FPS measurements or guarantees on physical GPU drivers. Additional mirror planes still require separate world renders; recursive reflections are unsupported.
